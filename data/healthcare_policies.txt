
POLICY PA-101: PRIOR AUTHORIZATION
Outpatient MRI requires prior authorization.
Emergency MRI does not require prior authorization.

POLICY CL-201: CLAIM SUBMISSION
Claims must be submitted within 90 days of the service date.

POLICY EL-301: MEMBER ELIGIBILITY
Members must have active coverage on the date of service.

POLICY COB-401: COORDINATION OF BENEFITS
When another insurer is primary, the primary insurer must process the claim before the secondary insurer.
